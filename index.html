      Adaptive calorie target

      A rough estimate of how much you burn each day, from what you logged and how your weight trend changed over the last 4 weeks (about 7,700 kcal per kg). Only days you logged count, so days that weren't fully logged, and short-term water changes, can throw it off. It's general guidance for adults, not medical advice: if you're under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, or managing a medical condition, talk to a health professional before changing your goal.
